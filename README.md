# STEP MDM DOCUMENTATION

This repository contains my notes, workflow documentation, and key learnings from working with Stibo STEP Master Data Management (MDM) during my internship at ISNIA Technologies (June–July 2025).

The goal of this repository is to document how enterprise organizations manage, validate, and distribute master data using the STEP platform, along with the concepts and workflows I explored during the internship.

About Stibo STEP

Stibo STEP is an enterprise-grade Master Data Management (MDM) platform that helps organizations maintain a single, trusted source of data across multiple business systems.

It is commonly used in industries such as:

Retail
Manufacturing
Supply Chain
E-Commerce

Some of its core capabilities include:

Centralized product information management
Data quality validation and governance
Workflow-driven approval processes
Multi-channel data distribution
Integration with ERP, PIM, and other enterprise systems

Why Master Data Management Matters

Organizations rely on accurate and consistent data to make business decisions. Poor-quality master data can lead to operational inefficiencies, reporting errors, and customer experience issues.

MDM platforms like STEP help organizations create a single source of truth for critical business data, improving data quality, governance, and operational efficiency across the enterprise.
